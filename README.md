> Most job platforms treat your resume like a PDF and your skills like a checkbox.  
> We don't do that here.

DevConnect Pro is a full-stack career platform built for developers — combining AI-powered resume tools, adaptive mock interviews, and a portfolio showcase, all in one place. Whether you're grinding for your first role or switching from backend to ML, this has your back.

---

## what it does

### 🧠 AI Resume Analyzer
Drop your resume in. Get real feedback — not the generic "use action verbs" type. The AI reads between the lines and tells you what's missing, what's weak, and what's actually working.

### 🎤 Interview Simulator

This is the part we're most proud of.

You get two ways to interview:

**1. Interview from your resume**  
The AI reads your resume and asks questions based on *your specific experience*. It's not a random Q&A — it actually references what you've written.

**2. Company-wise interviews**  
Pick a company track and a role. Currently available:

- 🤖 ML Engineer
- 🌐 Web Developer
- *(more coming)*

**3 interview difficulty modes:**

| Mode | What to expect |
|------|----------------|
| `Friendly ` | Conceptual and foundational questions |
| `Strict` | Applied problems, some system design |
| `Mentor` | Pressure, edge cases, back-to-back follow-ups |

### 🔁 Adaptive Interview AI

The interview doesn't follow a script. Your answers shape what comes next.

If you're breezing through, it pushes harder. If you're struggling, it adjusts. The difficulty adapts in real-time based on your performance — so every session is actually calibrated to you, not some average candidate.
